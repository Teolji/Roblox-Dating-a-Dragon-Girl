# 02 · Game design: *Hearthscale: A Kettlewick Story*

## Pitch
Your hot-air balloon crashes through the roof of Kettlewick's Lost & Found. Its keeper, a proud and dramatic fire dragon in humanoid form, logs you as lost property: **Item #4112**. To work off the roof you help her sort the hoard, return lost things and get ready for the Lanternfall Festival. Along the way you learn why she hasn't breathed real fire in eight years. Before Lanternfall night, you decide together what kind of flame you'll keep.

**Tone:** cozy, funny, sincere. The jokes come from character, the feelings are earned, and the stakes are personal rather than apocalyptic.

## Setting
**Kettlewick** is a small hillside village under **Hearthpeak**. On the summit burns the **Hearthflame**, an old beacon that holds off the Long Frost. Each autumn on **Lanternfall** night a dragon Warden rekindles it. This year it is guttering, and the only fire dragon in town insists she's "a tall woman with an extensive hat collection."

| Location | Use |
|---|---|

| **Village square** | Fountain, lanterns, notice board, festival tables (Ch. 4) |
| **Crumb's Bakery** | Mabel: gossip, gift hints, honey buns |

## Characters

### Saffron Ignavarre, Keeper of Lost Things (the dragon)
- **Who:** a fire dragon of 209, which is 26 in dragon years, so clearly an adult. Heir to the Hearthflame. She lives in humanoid form: tall, curling amber horns, copper-red scales on her cheeks and forearms, an expressive tail, round spectacles, a waistcoat and an apron full of pockets.
- **Personality:** proud, theatrical, precise (she has a Charter and a ledger), quick-witted and secretly anxious. She deflects with jokes. A puff of smoke escapes when she's flustered.
- **Goals:** to stop being afraid of her own fire; to give back what she has kept (her mother's lantern, and herself to the village); to decide where she belongs.
- **Flaws:** pride (won't ask for help); hoarding as control ("if I keep everything, nothing else gets lost"); avoidance.
- **Boundaries:** no touching her tail or horns without an invitation. She won't talk about her mother before she's ready. She dislikes being "fixed" and anything cold. Romance is only possible once she is open to it, **and she can say no**. If you confess with plenty of trust but not much romantic history, she tells you kindly that she sees you as a best friend.
- **Tells (animation):** fast tail-wag when happy; smoke, blush and a curled tail when flustered; smoke and a slow tail-thump when annoyed; drooping tail and head when sad.

### Supporting cast
| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| **Mabel Crumb** (baker) | Saffron's oldest friend. Gives gift hints, honey buns and gossip, and remembers Saffron's laugh |
| **Mayor Pimsworth "Pim" Thistlewhite** | Anxious festival organiser; comic relief. Gives you a Fire Starter Kit for her "little problem", a trap gift |
| **Wren Hollis** (young-adult apprentice lamplighter) | Curious about dragons; fixes your balloon (which lets you leave); tells you about the Old Bridge |
| **Cinderra Ignavarre** (the Eighth Flame) | Saffron's mother. Stern, ancient and kinder than she can show. She arrives to relight the flame and take Saffron home |

## Central conflict
- **External:** the Hearthflame will go out on Lanternfall night. If no one rekindles it, the Long Frost comes.
- **Internal:** eight years ago, during her Warden trial, Saffron lost control of her fire and burned the Old Bridge. Nobody was hurt, but the ceremony failed and her mother had to light the flame herself. Saffron fled, hid in the Lost & Found, and has made only smoke ever since.
- **Interpersonal:** what are you to each other, and will she go home with her mother?

## Story structure (first release)
| Ch. | Title | Beats | Key decision(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Item #4112* | Crash; meet Saffron; catalogue the hoard; return three strays; promotion to Assistant Keeper | First impression (kind, joking, curious or rude); respecting her "don't make it a whole thing" |
| 2 | *The Guttering Flame* | The Mayor's panic; asking about the flame; glowcaps; fishing at the pond (she tries to warm tea and makes only smoke); the Old Bridge story | Push her or respect her; **D1** at the bridge: comfort / honesty / insight / dismissal |
| 3 | *What the Hoard Remembers* | Her mother's summons; finding the Warden's lantern she buried in the hoard; fireflies and meteors | **D2**: return the lantern / keep it / carry it up the mountain; "do you think about staying?" |
| 4 | *Lanternfall Eve* | Cinderra lands in the square; Saffron hides under coats; painting lanterns; "What are we?" | **D3**: more than friends / best friends / "I'm leaving". Saffron's answer depends on trust and romance |
| 5 | *The Hearthflame* | The climb; the reconciliation (if you set it up); the finale | **D4**: together / I believe in you / with your mother / go home with her |
| — | Epilogue | Village reacts; NPC epilogue lines; endings gallery; *Start a new story* | — |

## Endings
| Ending | How you get it |
|---|---|
| **Two Flames** (romance) | Both of you chose romance in Ch. 4 (she accepts only with Trust ≥ 48 and Romance ≥ 12), and you don't send her home at the finale |
| **Kindred Sparks** (friendship) | The default for anyone she trusts. Variants: lit together with her mother, assisted, leaving in the balloon |
| **The Long Way Home** (bittersweet) | At the summit you tell her to go home and heal. Her mother lights the flame; she leaves you the Keeping House, and letters follow |
| **A Stranger's Lantern** (low trust) | Trust below 40 at the summit. She can't do it, and her mother lights the flame. Polite goodbye, with a hint at what was missed |

Earlier decisions also **change the epilogue paragraphs** (honesty at the bridge, reconciliation, how bright the flame burned) and **the finale gameplay**. Each point of Saffron's *Resolve*, earned through supportive choices, widens the timing window of the last minigame.

## Core loop
**Explore → talk → help (quest/activity) → unlock story events → deeper conversations**, repeated across five chapters.
1. The objective card and floating waypoint point to the next story beat.
2. Talking to NPCs runs branching scenes. A "!" marks a pending story scene.
3. Objectives ask you to collect, reach places, return items or share an activity.
4. Finishing a step is a *progress beat*. It lets you give Saffron one more gift and often opens new chat topics.
5. Milestones unlock as trust, affinity and story events line up.

## Systems

### Relationship model (not a single affection bar)
| Stat | Grows from | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust** | Respecting boundaries, honesty, keeping promises | Drops when you push, dismiss or give an insulting gift. Gates the finale |
| **Affinity** | Jokes, outings, chats, liked gifts | Gift and replay affinity is soft-capped per milestone |
| **Romance** | Romance-tagged choices once she's open to it | Romance edition only. Locked choices show *why* ("you don't feel close enough to say that") |
| **Her Resolve** | Supporting her courage | Changes the finale's difficulty |

**Milestones (never regress):** Unclaimed Item #4112 → **Assistant Keeper** (Ch. 1 quest) → **Friend** (pond heart-to-heart, Trust 22, Affinity 22; she asks you to call her *Saffron*) → **Confidant** (the full Old Bridge story, Trust 40, Affinity 36) → **Kindred Spirits** *or* **Sweethearts** (Ch. 4 decision, Trust 52, plus Romance 25 for Sweethearts). The Bond screen shows a live checklist for the next milestone.

### Gifts without grinding
- She accepts gifts only from Assistant Keeper onward, and only **one gift per progress beat** (a finished quest step or a first-time outing). There are no timers to wait out.
- Repeating an item gives half value, then nothing, with a unique "another one?" line.
- **Loved:** Smoked Ember Tea, the Lost Mitten, the Star Chart. **Liked:** Honey Bun, Glowcap, River Pebble. **Neutral:** Wildflowers. **Disliked:** Frostbloom (cold!). **Hated:** the Fire Starter Kit, which costs trust because it's a joke about her flame.
- Hints come from her own chat topics, from Mabel and from Wren. The journal remembers how she reacted to each gift.

### Shared activities (minigames)
| Activity | Kind | Where/when |
|---|---|---|
| Cataloguing the Hoard | Sort 8 items onto 4 shelves; answers stay server-side | Ch. 1; replayable |
| Fishing at Mirror Pond | Timing (5 rounds) | Ch. 2; replayable |
| Firefly Catching | Walk to glowing points in the world (server checks your position) | Ch. 3 on Stargazer Hill; replayable |
| Painting Festival Lanterns | Memory sequence (4 growing rounds) | Ch. 4; replayable |
| Rekindling the Hearthflame | Timing; Resolve widens the target | Finale |

The first clear gives the main reward, plus a tier bonus for a top score. Replays give a small soft-capped bonus, at most 3 times per activity. A low score never blocks the story.

### Quests, journal, world
- Quests are data: steps with objectives (talk, collect, reach, activity, condition). There are 11 quests across 5 chapters.
- The journal has **Quests**, **Story**, **Saffron** (facts you've learned and gift reactions) and **Endings** (a gallery you can re-read).
- Each player has their own world: NPC stations, pickups, time of day and the Hearthflame's brightness all follow **that player's** story, even on a shared server.

## Interface and controls
- **HUD:** objective card (tap for the journal), buttons for Journal, Bag, Bond and Settings (touch-sized, with key hints J/B/K/P), and a save indicator.
- **Dialogue:** typewriter text (skippable), portrait and nameplate, choices as large buttons. Number keys 1–4, gamepad D-pad and A, or tap. Narration is in italics; romance choices carry a ♥.
- **Settings:** text speed, music and SFX volume, interface size, objective marker, cinematic camera, reduce motion, show exact numbers, start a new story.
- **Mobile:** Roblox thumbstick and jump, tap-to-interact prompts, scale-aware text, no hover-only UI.
- **Gamepad:** ProximityPrompt (X), dialogue (A/B/D-pad), menus on the D-pad, B to close.

## Visual direction (practical for Roblox import)
- **Style:** stylised and chunky, with soft bevels and a warm palette (ember orange, parchment, moss green, dusk blue). Readable silhouettes over detail. Smooth plastic plus a few hero materials (wood, slate, fabric).
- **Saffron:** tall (about 1.1× a default character) with big curling amber horns, cheek and forearm scale plates, a thick tapering tail, and round spectacles. Modest clothes: shirt, waistcoat, rolled sleeves, pocket apron, boots. Expressive face (eyes, brows, mouth) and an animated tail.
- **Budget per character:** under 10k triangles, one or two 1024² textures, an R15-compatible skinned rig plus 4–5 tail bones. Details in [05-Assets.md](05-Assets.md).
- **Lighting:** the time of day moves with the story (afternoon → dusk → night → Lanternfall night → morning epilogue). The Hearthflame on the peak acts as a visible progress meter.

## Audio direction
Acoustic and folk: plucked strings, a soft accordion, music-box motifs for Saffron, low brass for Cinderra. Night tracks for the hill, a rising build for the finale. SFX: page flips, a ledger stamp, smoke puffs and fire whoosh, lantern chimes. **No audio ships with this project**; see [05-Assets.md](05-Assets.md).
